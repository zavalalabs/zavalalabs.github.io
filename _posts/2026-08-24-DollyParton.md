It's hard to find the words for how I'm feeling today, learning that Dolly Parton has passed away. I want to take a moment to join the countless people around the world sharing their condolences and their gratitude for how much she meant to them.

Early in my career, during the most stressful stretch of my move into technology, I used to set my alarm for 9:00. Every morning before the hardest appointments and the hardest work, Dolly would wake me up with her music. It feels almost unreal that just the other night I was watching her in *Unlikely Angel*, and I think that's part of why the news is hitting harder today. It's also hard not to notice that she passed on the anniversary of my seventh year at my current employer, a day I've marked more than once by humming "9 to 5" and telling myself, in her words, to pour myself a cup of ambition.

It saddens me that we've lost someone so iconic, but Dolly worked hard her entire life, and she has earned her rest.

Her music has been part of my home for as long as I can remember, from my father's generation to just yesterday. We played her records, watched her films, and loved her humor, her light, and her laughter. Her duets with Kenny Rogers were always a favorite. The world feels a little more incomplete without her in it.

I never got to see Dolly perform live, but I still feel connected to her through her music, through the hearts of her fans, and through the good work she leaves behind.

With all the love and respect in the world, I hope her family and friends find peace in knowing how loved she was, and that her music will live on forever. Rest in peace, Dolly Parton. You will be missed but never forgotten.
