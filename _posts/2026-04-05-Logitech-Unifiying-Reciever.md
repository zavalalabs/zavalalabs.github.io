The IT landscape, no matter your position, has a lot of things that get returned to your office. One of the things that will always find its way back to you is the Logitech Unifying Receiver. This small USB device is used to connect multiple Logitech peripherals, such as mice and keyboards, to a single computer. 

When you receive a Logitech Unifying Receiver, it's important to check if it is still functional and if it can be paired with any devices. You can do this by using the Logitech Unifying Software on macos or in windows, even linux people have no reason to not be able to do a simple check. On linux load up the solaar application and check whats paired to the reciver first go. 

Why does this matter? Here is the thing, if the reciever is still good working order but the keyboard and/or mouse or other "unifying" connected device is not, you can re-use this reciver other places with the other logitech or some dell branded peripherals. Use the software to pair and unpair devices as needed. I have seen far too many of these get tossed into ewaste or worse thrown into the trash becase people have not taeknt the time to learn about how this little device works. 

There are countelss times ive seen the most senior of sysadmin not even  know that they can be moved around and fixed up with other perhperals in the stock pile. So please, for the sake of being a better admin, take the time to leanr this. Even by reading my little rant here, you have taken a first step in learning what is probably sitting righ on your desk.
