Visual Pass Criteria (check manually)

  • All node labels are readable — light text on dark background, no white-on-white
  • Arrow/edge lines are visible against the dark page background
  • Node borders are visible but not harsh or glowing
  • No large white or near-white bounding boxes on any node
  • Hierarchy levels are distinguishable (CEO/C-suite vs Directors vs Managers vs ICs)
  • Chart does not overflow or clip on a 1440px viewport
